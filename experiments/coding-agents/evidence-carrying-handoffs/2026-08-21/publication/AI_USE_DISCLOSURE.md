# AI-use disclosure

OpenAI Codex was used during construction of this public protocol preview for:

- literature discovery and organization;
- methodological critique and threat modeling;
- drafting and editing documentation;
- drafting schemas, validation code, and automated tests;
- running repository checks and reporting their outputs.

The human author directed the research question, positioning, scope, and
publication decision and remains responsible for every claim and artifact.
Scientific references in the preview were checked against primary publication
pages or official proceedings. Executable claims are backed by repository tests
rather than by the model's assessment.

No study-model trajectory or outcome was generated for this preview. AI output
was not used as an experimental observation, primary evaluator, participant
annotation, or substitute for the planned independent executable evaluator.
Any later use of AI in handoff generation or annotation must be frozen as part
of the treatment or measurement process and recorded in the run manifest.
